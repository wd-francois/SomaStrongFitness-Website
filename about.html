About me

Hi, I'm Kathy.

I'm a certified personal trainer with over ten years of experience running my own personal training business. Health and fitness have always been a passion of mine — but what truly drives me is helping women discover their own strength, both physically and mentally.

Over the years, I've worked with women of all ages, backgrounds, and fitness levels. Whether it's lifting their first weight, building confidence in movement, or creating sustainable healthy habits, I'm deeply invested in every client's journey. There's nothing more rewarding than seeing my clients achieve goals they once thought were out of reach.

My approach is supportive, practical, and results-focused. I believe fitness should be empowering — not intimidating — and I've created a private home studio that reflects that: a welcoming space where you can show up as you are, and grow into your strongest, healthiest self.

Let's build something strong — together.

For expecting & new mothers

Strength training for pre & postnatal women

Safe, effective, and empowering strength training programs tailored specifically for women during and after pregnancy. I understand the unique physiological changes that occur during this transformative time and I'm here to support your body, mind, and goals — whether you're preparing for childbirth or rebuilding your strength postpartum.

For expecting mothers (prenatal)

  • 🌿 Improves posture & reduces back pain — pregnancy can shift your centre of gravity and strain your lower back; strength training helps support your spine and posture.
  • 💪 Builds stamina for labour & delivery — a stronger body can lead to a smoother labour experience and quicker recovery.
  • 🧠 Boosts energy & mood — exercise helps reduce stress, anxiety, and fatigue, supporting mental wellbeing.
  • 🩸 Supports healthy weight gain and circulation — staying active reduces the risk of gestational diabetes and swelling.

For new mothers (postnatal)

  • 🔄 Promotes faster recovery — gentle strength work helps restore core function and pelvic floor strength.
  • 🧍‍♀️ Improves posture and reduces muscle imbalances — carrying and feeding your baby often creates tension; training corrects and prevents discomfort.
  • 💥 Supports mental health & confidence — exercise releases endorphins, helps manage postnatal depression, and restores a sense of control and empowerment.
  • 🤱 Boosts energy for motherhood — strength training improves functional fitness so you can meet the demands of motherhood with more ease.

Safe & supportive environment for women

Every pregnancy and postpartum journey is different, so programs are:

  • 🧑‍⚕️ Designed in consultation with women's health professionals
  • 📋 Tailored to your trimester or postpartum stage
  • 🫶 Offered in a private, non-intimidating setting exclusively for women

Real habits. Real results. No diets.

Nutrition coaching for women

At SomaStrong Fitness, lasting change doesn't come from restriction — it comes from building sustainable, healthy habits that support your lifestyle, your goals, and your wellbeing. This coaching is designed specifically for women who are ready to feel better in their bodies, improve their relationship with food, and finally ditch the all-or-nothing mindset.

What makes this approach different?

  • 🌀 No meal plans. No calorie obsession.

    No strict meal plans, no counting every bite — instead, we work together to understand your unique needs and build habits around real food, balance, and flexibility.

  • 💬 1:1 support & accountability

    Personalised guidance, check-ins, and coaching that meet you where you are — and evolve as you do.

  • 🌿 Habit-based method

    Simple, powerful shifts: eating more mindfully, balancing meals with protein, fibre, and healthy fats, staying hydrated, listening to hunger and fullness cues, and planning meals without overwhelm.

  • ⏳ Sustainable for life

    Not a 30-day challenge — a way of eating that supports your energy, hormones, and lifestyle for the long run.

Is this right for you?

This habit-based nutrition coaching is ideal if:

  • You're tired of yo-yo dieting and want real change
  • You want to feel in control around food
  • You're supporting your fitness goals without obsession
  • You're navigating pregnancy, postpartum, perimenopause, or menopause
  • You value a compassionate, supportive approach to wellness
