About me

Hi, I'm Kathy, a certified personal trainer with over ten years of experience running my own personal training business. Health and fitness have always been a passion of mine—but what truly drives me is helping women discover their own strength, both physically and mentally. Over the years, I've worked with women of all ages, backgrounds, and fitness levels. Whether it's lifting their first weight, building confidence in movement, or creating sustainable healthy habits, I'm deeply invested in every client's journey. There's nothing more rewarding than seeing my clients achieve goals they once thought were out of reach. My approach is supportive, practical, and results-focused. I believe fitness should be empowering—not intimidating—and I've created a private home studio that reflects that: a welcoming space where you can show up as you are, and grow into your strongest, healthiest self. Let's build something strong—together. Why Strength Training Matters During & After Pregnancy For Expecting Mothers (Prenatal): • 🌿 Improves posture & reduces back pain Pregnancy can shift your center of gravity and strain your lower back. Strength training helps support your spine and posture. • 💪 Builds stamina for labor & delivery A stronger body can lead to a smoother labor experience and quicker recovery. • 🧠 Boosts energy & mood Exercise helps reduce stress, anxiety, and fatigue, supporting mental wellbeing. • 🩸 Supports healthy weight gain and circulation Staying active reduces the risk of gestational diabetes and swelling. For New Mothers (Postnatal): • 🔄 Promotes faster recovery Gentle strength work helps restore core function and pelvic floor strength. • 🧍‍♀️ Improves posture and reduces muscle imbalances Carrying and feeding your baby often creates tension—training corrects and prevents discomfort. • 💥 Supports mental health & confidence Exercise releases endorphins, helps manage postnatal depression, and restores a sense of control and empowerment. • 🤱 Boosts energy for motherhood Strength training improves functional fitness so you can meet the demands of motherhood with more ease.
